Support

We're here to help.

Contact us

Email blaszen.dev@gmail.com and we'll reply within 2 business days.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a reset link.

Report content or a user

Open the recipe and tap the flag icon to report it, or open the chef's profile and tap block. We review reports within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the app go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, recipes, lists, favorites, friends, lesson progress and photos.

Recipe search or AI not working?

Check your connection and try again in a minute. AI recipes are limited per day; if it keeps failing, email us.
