Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

What we collect

Your email address and password (handled by Firebase Authentication), the display name and profile details you choose to add, your profile photo, the recipes, photos, grocery and prep lists you create, your favorites, friend connections, chat messages and Masterclass lesson progress.

How we use it

To run your account and show your content in the app. Public recipes are visible to other signed-in users in the Community Feed; private recipes are visible only to you. Your display name, email and photo are visible to other users so they can find and connect with you.

Service providers

Data is stored with Google Firebase (authentication, database and file storage). Recipe searches are sent to Spoonacular through our server; your account details are not shared with Spoonacular.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show third-party ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, recipes, lists, favorites, friend connections, lesson progress and uploaded photos. Messages you sent in shared chats may remain visible to the other person.

Questions about privacy? Email blaszen.dev@gmail.com.
